# 📐 Guess the Hypotenuse – Group Challenge

An interactive HTML/CSS/JavaScript app that turns learning the Pythagorean theorem into a collaborative group activity.  
Perfect for classrooms, study groups, or pair programming – students work together to **guess the hypotenuse** without seeing it first.

![App screenshot](screenshot.png) *(optional)*

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This tool is designed to **reinforce the relationship** `a² + b² = c²` in a fun, engaging way. Instead of passively reading the theorem, students actively:

- Adjust the legs **a** and **b** using sliders.
- **Calculate** the hypotenuse **c** mentally or on paper.
- **Enter their guess** and receive immediate feedback.
- **Discuss** strategies and compare results with peers.

The app hides the value of **c** until the group decides to reveal it – encouraging critical thinking and collaboration.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive triangle** – drawn in real time as you move the sliders.
- **Hidden hypotenuse** – `c` is never shown in the sliders, formula box, or canvas until you click “Show answer”.
- **Formula hints** – displays `a²`, `b²`, and `a² + b²` to help students work out `c`.
- **3 student slots** – each can enter their own guess and check it independently.
- **Instant feedback** – each guess is evaluated as **Perfect**, **Close**, or **Wrong** (without revealing the exact answer).
- **Reveal button** – shows the true value of `c` when the group is ready.
- **Randomise** – click on the triangle to generate new random values for `a` and `b`.
- **Responsive design** – works on desktops, tablets, and phones.

---

## 🧑‍🏫 How to use

1. **Open the app** – it runs entirely in your browser (no server needed).
2. **Adjust the legs** – use the sliders to set values for `a` and `b`. The triangle will redraw.
3. **Each student** types their guess for `c` in their own input field and clicks **Check**.
4. **Read the feedback** – each student sees how close their guess is.
5. **Discuss** – why did some guesses land closer? What mental math did they use?
6. **Reveal** – when the group agrees, click **Show answer** to see the exact value of `c`.
7. **Repeat** – adjust the sliders or click on the triangle for a new challenge.
