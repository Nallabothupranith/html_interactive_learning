A five-stage walk through quadratic polynomials for NCERT Class X Chapter 2.
Students drag sliders on a live, auto-scaling parabola to see how the
coefficients a, b and c change its shape, zeroes, and vertex; learn the
relationship between zeroes and coefficients; practise forming a quadratic
from its zeroes step by step; take a quick multiple-choice challenge; and
finish with two self-contained CBSE-style case studies (a fountain's water
path and a parabolic arch) that need no outside reference material.
