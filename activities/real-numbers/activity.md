Three linked mini-tools for NCERT Class X Chapter 1 (Real Numbers). Students
find the HCF of two numbers step by step with Euclid's Division Algorithm,
build a prime-factorisation ladder for two numbers to derive HCF and LCM from
the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, and test any fraction to predict -
before dividing - whether its decimal expansion terminates or repeats, based
on the prime factors of the denominator.

A second, five-stage variation (Explore, Learn, Guided Practice, Challenge
Zone, Exam Practice) walks through the same HCF/LCM/prime-factorisation
skills with a self-paced step-by-step format, a quick multiple-choice
challenge, and two self-contained CBSE-style case studies.
