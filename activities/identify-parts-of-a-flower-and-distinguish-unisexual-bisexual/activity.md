title: Identify Parts of a Flower and Distinguish Unisexual/Bisexual
---

Cut a flower (like Petunia) vertically to identify sepals, petals, stamens, and pistil. Flowers with both stamens and pistil are bisexual; flowers with only one are unisexual.
